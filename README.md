# 📺 Bright TV Viewership Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses Bright TV's customer viewership data to uncover behavioural patterns, audience demographics, channel performance, and viewing trends. The objective is to provide data-driven recommendations that help Bright TV grow its subscription base through Customer Value Management (CVM).

The project follows the complete analytics lifecycle—from data inspection and cleaning to SQL analysis, dashboard development, business insights, and executive recommendations.

## Business Problem

Bright TV aims to increase its subscriber base during the current financial year.

Using customer profiles and viewing transaction data, this project identifies:

- Customer viewing behaviour
- Audience demographics
- Content consumption trends
- High-performing and underperforming channels
- Opportunities to improve customer engagement
- Strategic recommendations for subscriber growth

## Objectives

- Analyse customer viewing behaviour
- Understand audience demographics
- Identify the most popular TV channels
- Discover weekly and daily viewing patterns
- Evaluate geographic audience distribution
- Identify growth opportunities
- Produce executive-level dashboards and recommendations

## Dataset

The dataset contains approximately:

- 1,000+ customer viewing records
- 17 television channels
- 9 South African provinces
- 3 months of viewing activity

The data includes:

- User demographics
- Age
- Gender
- Province
- Race
- Channel viewed
- Date and time viewed
- Viewing duration

## Dashboard Insights

The analysis answers several key business questions including:

### Channel Performance
- Most watched channels by month
- Highest growth channels
- Underperforming channels

### Audience Analysis
- Largest age groups
- Customer demographics
- Audience segmentation

### Weekly Viewing Patterns
- Most active viewing days
- Peak viewing periods
- Weekend engagement trends

### Geographic Analysis
- Viewership by province
- Gender distribution
- Regional opportunities

### Daypart Analysis
- Morning vs Afternoon vs Evening performance
- Late-night viewing opportunities

## Key Findings

- Sports content is the strongest engagement driver.
- March recorded the highest overall viewership.
- Youth represent the largest customer segment.
- Gauteng has the highest concentration of viewers.
- Female viewers remain significantly underrepresented.
- Kids and pensioners present untapped growth opportunities.
- Late-night viewing has the greatest potential for programming improvements.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Invest in additional sports broadcasting rights.
- Refresh underperforming entertainment channels.
- Expand children's programming.
- Introduce content targeting pensioners.
- Develop more female-focused programming.
- Increase engagement during low-viewership periods.
